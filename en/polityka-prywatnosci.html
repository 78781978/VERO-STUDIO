Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

1. Data controller

The controller of personal data collected through this website is Wioletta Pazdańska, operating under the brand Vero Studio, based in Kraków, Poland. Contact for data protection matters: info@verostudio.pl or by phone: +48 573 377 330.

2. What data we collect and why

We only collect personal data that you provide yourself, in particular via the contact form on the Contact page. Data collected: full name, email address, and message content. We process this data to respond to your inquiry, prepare a quote, and, where applicable, enter into and perform a service agreement.

3. Legal basis for processing

  • Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, consent given when submitting the contact form,
  • Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, steps taken prior to entering into a contract and its performance, at your request,
  • Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in responding to inquiries addressed to us.

4. Recipients of data

Messages submitted via the contact form are delivered through a third-party form-processing provider (e.g. Formspree or EmailJS), acting as a data processor on our behalf. Your data may be processed on servers located outside the European Economic Area, in which case the provider ensures an adequate level of data protection in line with GDPR requirements (e.g. via standard contractual clauses). Beyond this, we do not share your data with other parties unless required by law.

5. Data retention period

Data submitted through the contact form is stored for as long as necessary to handle your inquiry, and, if a business relationship is established, for the duration of the agreement plus any period required by law (e.g. tax or accounting regulations). If no cooperation follows, we delete the data no later than 12 months after the last contact.

6. Your rights

In relation to the processing of your personal data, you have the right to:

  • access your data and receive a copy of it,
  • rectify (correct) your data,
  • erase your data,
  • restrict processing,
  • object to processing,
  • data portability,
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal,
  • lodge a complaint with the Polish Data Protection Authority (UODO) if you believe processing violates GDPR.

To exercise the above rights, contact us at info@verostudio.pl.

7. Cookies and tracking technologies

In its current version, the website does not use cookies or any analytics or marketing tools that track visitor traffic. If we implement such tools in the future (e.g. Google Analytics), we will disclose this in an updated version of this policy and, where required, request your consent.

8. Fonts and external resources

All fonts and graphic assets used on this website are hosted directly on our own server. The site does not connect to external servers (e.g. Google Fonts) for this purpose, so your IP address is not shared with external providers while browsing the site.

9. Automated decision-making

We do not use automated decision-making, including profiling, that would produce legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly affect you.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated periodically, e.g. following changes in law or how the website operates. The current version is always available at this address.
